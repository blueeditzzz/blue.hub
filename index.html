About

Who I am →

I build interfaces that feel alive — clean structure underneath, deliberate motion on top.

I'm Harshit, a web developer and app developer. My work sits where engineering meets design: responsive layouts, considered typography, and interactions that make a product feel considered rather than assembled.

I'm currently in my first year of B.Tech at GLA University, Mathura, and Microsoft Certified. Alongside coursework I spend my time building on the web, exploring app development, and studying modern UI/UX patterns from the products I admire.

How this account works

Read this before you rely on it for anything important.

Real accounts, real sessions. Sign-up, sign-in and password recovery run through Supabase Auth over HTTPS, so your password never touches this page or this browser's storage. Your profile is one row in a database table that only your account can read or change, and your picture sits in a storage folder only your account can write to. Sessions refresh automatically; Remember me keeps you signed in on this device until you sign out.

Account management

Deleting removes your account, its profile and its picture from Supabase. It can't be undone.
