Password & security

Changing your password re-hashes it with a new salt. Old passwords are never stored or shown.

How this account works

Read this before you rely on it for anything important.

Frontend-only demo. Your account is a JSON record in this browser's localStorage, and your picture sits in IndexedDB. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 (150,000 iterations, random salt), so no plain-text password is ever written — but anyone with access to this device can edit the stored data directly. This is not secure authentication and it is not a real login gateway. Accounts do not sync between browsers or devices, and no email is ever sent.

Account management

Deleting removes this account, its profile and its picture from this browser. It can't be undone.
